How it works

  1. Paste a link. Any public YouTube tutorial: code, recipes, yoga flows, crafts.

  2. Gemini maps the steps. It watches the whole video and splits it into steps, each with what finished looks like.

  3. Your turn. The video pauses after each step. Share your laptop screen for code, or point your phone camera at your work for everything else.

  4. Coached out loud. It checks your work, tells you what to fix, and moves on once it looks right.

What it does

Like a tutor sitting next to you

It stays quiet while you work and speaks up when it matters: when you're done, when something's off and you've stopped, or when you ask.

Waits for you

The video stops at the end of every step and only moves on when your work matches it. No more pause, rewind, pause.
